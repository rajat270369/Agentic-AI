1. ## AI is not given the meanings of words.



## 2\. It learns meaning by observing patterns in huge amounts of text.



## 3\. Words used in similar contexts tend to develop similar embeddings.



## 4\. Embeddings are learned, not manually assigned.



## 5\. Similar embeddings help the model generalize to new situations.





## Questions-





1. ### Why doesn't an AI need someone to manually define every word?



#### Ans- The AI doesn't need someone to manually define every word because it learns word meanings by observing how words are used in millions or billions of sentences. Embeddings are the result of that learning, not the reason it learns.



### 2\. If "cat" and "dog" often appear in similar contexts, why would their embeddings become similar?



#### Ans- Because through embedding those words will get a similar vector and then we will do a dot product of both of them the result is also gonna tell us how similar they are 



### 3\. Why is it useful for your AI Operating System if related concepts are close together in embedding space?



#### Ans- So that it can reason better and understand the similarities between words. Which are essential for this operating system. 



### 4\. If you introduced a completely new word into the language tomorrow, what do you think the model would need in order to eventually learn its meaning?



#### Ans- It would simply need embedding so that the word will have a meaning in the computer language. It could also have Unicode or ASCII however those things are outdated compared to embedding. 



### 5\. In your own words, explain the difference between a token ID and an embedding vector.



#### Ans- The difference is that token ID is just a number which is represented for that specific character, word or sentence. While a embedding vector doesn't just store a random unique number for a specific text. it stores it's meaning.
